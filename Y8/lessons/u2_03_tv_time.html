Screen Time & TV Checker

Make
How much TV is too much? Use a 3-way branch to judge daily viewing hours!
Your Mission
  1. Ask the user how many hours of TV they watch each day (int).
  2. If less than 2: print That should be ok.
  3. If between 2 and 4 (inclusive): print That will rot your brain.
  4. Otherwise: print That is too much TV!
Key Pattern
elif tv_time >= 2 and tv_time <= 4:
    print("That will rot your brain")
👀 Predict First! Click to check what happens

1 gives That should be ok, 3 gives That will rot your brain, and 6 gives That is too much TV!

💡 Stuck? Common Bug Hint

Check tv_time < 2 first, then tv_time <= 4 (or tv_time >= 2 and tv_time <= 4), then else:.
