Capstone: The House & Village Builder

Make
Final Year 8 Boss Level: combine your subroutines to build a whole street of houses (or a sky of growing stars)!
Your Mission
  1. Challenge 1 (House & Village): Write draw_square() and draw_triangle() (tip: turn left 120° for the roof so it sits on top of the square!), then use a loop to draw 3 houses in a row.
  2. Bonus Challenge (Variable Star): Write draw_star(size) (5 points, turning 144°) and loop it 5 times making size bigger each time.
Key Pattern
def draw_house():
    draw_square()
    draw_triangle()
👀 Predict First! Click to check what happens

Calling draw_square() then draw_triangle() (turning left) places a neat pitched roof right on top of the square!

💡 Stuck? Common Bug Hint

Remember to use penup(), move forward, and pendown() between houses in your village loop.
