Turtle Square with a FOR Loop

PredictRunModify
Instead of writing forward and right four times, let a for loop do the heavy lifting!
Your Mission
  1. Predict & Run: Watch the turtle draw a square.
  2. Modify: Try changing the loop count and angle to draw an equilateral triangle (3 sides, 120° turn) or pentagon (5 sides, 72° turn).
  3. Angle Cheat-Sheet: Triangle = 120, Square = 90, Pentagon = 72, Hexagon = 60, Octagon = 45.
Key Pattern
import turtle
for i in range(4):
    turtle.forward(100)
    turtle.right(90)
👀 Predict First! Click to check what happens

The turtle moves forward 100 pixels and turns right 90 degrees four times, completing a closed square.

💡 Stuck? Common Bug Hint

External angle always equals 360 / number_of_sides!
