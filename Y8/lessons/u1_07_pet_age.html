Pet Age Calculator

Make
How old is your dog really? Build a quick converter using the classic ×7 rule.
Your Mission
  1. Ask the user for their dog's age in human years using int(input(...)).
  2. Multiply by 7 and store it in dog_years.
  3. Print: Your dog is <X> years old in dog years!
Key Pattern
human_years = int(input("How old is your dog in human years? "))
dog_years = human_years * 7
👀 Predict First! Click to check what happens

If the user enters 4, the output will include 28.

💡 Stuck? Common Bug Hint

Remember to wrap input(...) in int(...) before multiplying by 7.
