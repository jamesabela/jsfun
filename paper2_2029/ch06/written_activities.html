Written activities and answers

Planning worksheet

A school runs a uniform shop. Every shirt costs 12. A single delivery fee of 3 applies to an order. Input shirt quantity and output the total.

  1. Identify essential information and two irrelevant details.
  2. Separate the task into input, process, output and storage.
  3. State assumptions about quantity.
  4. Calculate the expected result for 4 shirts.
  5. Extend the model so collection from school has no delivery fee. Identify the additional input.

Model answers

Quantity, unit price and delivery rule are essential. Shirt display-rack colour and the cashier's favourite game do not affect this calculation. Input quantity; multiply by 12 and add 3; output total; store quantity and total in variables and fixed charges in constants. Assume a positive integer quantity unless the requirements permit zero or require validation. Four shirts cost 51. Add a collection/delivery choice and apply the fee only for delivery. Abstraction selects relevant data; decomposition separates the operations.
