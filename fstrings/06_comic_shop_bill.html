F-String Comic Academy - Level 6 of 10

Comic Shop Bill

Two decimal places

Comic briefing
The shop total says 7.5. It looks unfinished.
Use :.2f to always show two digits after the decimal point.

What you will learn

Format a money value to two decimal places.

The format code :.2f means display a floating-point number with exactly two digits after the decimal point.

Your mission

Calculate the total and print the bill with exactly two decimal places.

Code pattern

price = 4.2
print(f"Price: ${price:.2f}")

Mission checklist

  • Calculate price * quantity.
  • Use total inside the f-string.
  • Add :.2f after the variable name.

Common bug

The colon goes inside the braces: {total:.2f}.

Run the code, test it, then unlock the next comic mission.
